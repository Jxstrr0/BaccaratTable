Punto Banco

Bet on which hand finishes closer to 9: Player or Banker. Cards 2–9 count at face value, aces count 1, and tens and court cards count 0. Only the last digit of the total counts, so 7 + 8 = 5.

The dealer follows fixed drawing rules. Either hand totalling 8 or 9 on two cards is a natural, and the coup ends there. Otherwise Player draws on 0–5, and Banker draws according to the tableau.

The squeeze. As the only player, you hold the largest bet, so the dealer passes you the cards of the side you backed. Peel each one up slowly from an edge to see the pips before it turns over.
